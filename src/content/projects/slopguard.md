Automated supply-chain security scanner. slopguard inspects a project's dependency tree and flags packages that look risky before they ever get installed -- typosquats, sketchy maintainer changes, and other supply-chain red flags.

Phase 1 (the MVP) is shipped. Phase 3 -- a much larger test corpus to validate detection accuracy against real-world attacks -- is next.

Built as a portfolio piece aimed squarely at security-engineering roles.
