A self-coaching tracker for badminton. rally-coach turns practice footage into feedback: it tracks the shuttle and your pose through a drill, then shows you where your form breaks down.

Currently tracking drill-mode footage -- hanging-shuttle drills where the full body isn't always in frame -- using a vendored copy of MonoTrack's tracking model as the detection backbone.
